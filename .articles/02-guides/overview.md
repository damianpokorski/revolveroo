`http://localhost/overview` is a read-only page for checking on the whole set-up from one place. It updates live.

![The overview page with attention items, display cards and a tabs by displays grid.](../../../assets/screenshots/overview.png)

It shows three things:

- **Needs a look**: anything that needs attention, such as a tab that targets no display, an offline display or a blank frame.
- **A card per display**: its current tab and whether it is online.
- **A tabs by displays grid**: exactly where every tab lands across every display, given each tab's **Shows on** and **When** rules.

Use it after changing a rule to confirm the tab now plays where you meant it to.
